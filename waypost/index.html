                    Open

                    Server (mailbox)

                    A WordPress site with the Waypost plugin receives the reports. Its address and keys are under Settings › Waypost on that site.

                    Map and theme
                    Interface theme

                    Your basemap, theme and panel choices are remembered on this device. Place search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim; road routes use OSRM.

                    Offline maps

                    Save the map of a small area while you have signal, and it still shows when you don't. Move and zoom the map to the area first.

                      Areas are saved slowly and kept small (up to 2,500 map tiles each) to stay within fair use. Make sure your use fits the map provider's terms. OpenStreetMap's own servers, Google and Esri don't allow saving.

                      Add your own map source

                      For a map server your organisation runs or has a licence for. The address must have {z}, {x} and {y} in it, for example https://tiles.example.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png
