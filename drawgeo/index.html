      Area unit

      Only changes how areas are shown. Exported files always store area_m2 in square metres. Assam: 1 bigha = 5 katha = 100 lessa = 14,400 sq ft.

      Colour shapes by

      Colours lines, areas and points by one field, with a legend on the map. Click a legend entry to select those features.

      Offline map

      Optional. Saves the basemap for the area on screen before you go where there is no signal. Esri basemaps are the safe choice: saving OpenStreetMap or Google maps breaks the OpenStreetMap tile policy and Google's terms, so DrawGeo warns and asks first.

      Help

      Edit a GeoJSON file: under GeoJSON file, press Open file… or drop a file on the map. Its shapes go on the map and the attribute table opens. Change values, add, rename or delete fields, draw or delete features, edit shapes, then Save (Ctrl+S) writes back to the same file, keeping its feature order and any extra content. Chrome and Edge write to the file directly; other browsers download a new copy.

      Attribute table: click a cell and type to replace it, or press Enter or double-click to change it. Enter and Tab save and move on; Esc cancels; Delete empties the cell. Shift- or Ctrl-click rows, or shapes on the map, to select several, then use Set value or Delete. Paste a block copied from a spreadsheet into the table. Search finds any value; area_m2 > 1000, village = Majuli or name ~ plot filter by one field. The ▾ in each column header sorts, renames, changes the type, labels the map or deletes the field. Form shows one feature at a time, which suits phones. Ctrl+Z undoes table changes.

      Edit shape (in the table, the form or a row's right-click menu) moves the points of just that feature: drag a handle to move it, drag a faint middle handle to add one, click a handle to remove it, then press Done.

      Type point… (while drawing): enter coordinates (decimal or degrees-minutes-seconds), or a distance and bearing from the last point, for traverse surveys from a field book. Add part and Cut hole work on the selected shape: draw the extra part, or the hole inside an area.

      Average a point (under Walk with GPS): stand still while DrawGeo averages 10 to 120 GPS readings. If you are drawing a line or area, the average becomes its next corner; otherwise it becomes a new point with its spread recorded.

      Undo and Redo (also Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) cover drawing, Move points, Remove, Continue last line, imports and every table change. While drawing, Ctrl+Z removes the last point.

      Calculate fills a field from an expression, for example round(area_m2 / 1337.8, 2), concat(village, '-', plot_no) or if(area_m2 > 1000, 'large', 'small'). $area, $length and $perimeter are measured on the WGS 84 ellipsoid. The bottom row of the table shows totals for the rows shown; click a total to switch between sum, mean, minimum, maximum and count. Date fields accept 24/10/2026, 2026-10-24 or 24 Oct 2026 and are stored as 2026-10-24.

      Sides: the Form view lists every side of a line or area with its length and bearing; Copy puts them on the clipboard for a spreadsheet.

      Line: click to add points; click the last point or press Finish to end.

      Area: click to add corners; click the first point or press Finish to close it.

      Point: click once where it belongs.

      Each new shape gets a suggested unique name. Rename it later from the Features list.

      Measurements: every area gets area_m2 and perimeter_m; every line gets length_m and bearing_deg (the direction from its first point to its last, clockwise from north). They are computed on the WGS 84 ellipsoid. They are shown live while drawing and updated whenever a shape is changed, and they are saved in projects and exports.

      Snapping (optional): new or moved points stick to the nearest point, or else the nearest edge, of another shape within a short distance. Use it for shared boundaries.

      Walk with GPS: press Start walk and walk the path or boundary. Points are added as you move, skipping readings less accurate than the limit you chose. Finish as a line or an area. If the app closes, the walk is offered again when you return.

      Pen tip helps on phones: with a draw tool active, touch the map and a pen appears with its point above your finger. Slide to aim (coordinates show on the map), lift to mark. Lift on the last point (line) or first point (area) to finish. Two fingers pan and zoom.

      Projects: keep as many named projects as you like in this browser. Open project lists them to open, rename or delete. Restore previous brings back the map from before your last New project or Open project.

      Keyboard: Esc cancels drawing, Ctrl+S saves the open file (or the project when no file is open), Ctrl+Shift+S saves as, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo table changes.
