Instructions & Guide

Use the draw toolbar (top-left) to create:

  • Line: click to add vertices; click the last point or Finish to end
  • Area: draw closed polygons; click the first point to close
  • Point: small circular markers

Each new feature gets a suggested unique name, which you can change.

Pen tip (button under the draw toolbar), for precise marking on phones:

  • Tap it to switch Off → Ballpoint → Pencil
  • With a draw tool active, touch the map: a pen appears with its point above your finger
  • Slide to aim (coordinates show at the bottom), lift to mark the point
  • Lift on the last point (line) or first point (area) to finish; two fingers pan/zoom

Menu (bottom-left):

  • New / Load / Save Project: one project is saved in this browser
  • Save GeoJSON: export to a file
  • Restore Previous: bring back the map from before your last New Project or Load
  • Resume Line, Stop Editing, Zoom to Data

Your work is autosaved as you draw. The autosave is cleared once you save the project or export all layers.
