Evaluator extension

This extension sends what you select, the page you are on, or a screenshot to your Evaluator app, which then checks it against the sources you trust, with your own keys. The extension itself reads nothing until you use it, and sends nothing anywhere else.

The address you open Evaluator at, for example the one your school or organisation gave you. For a copy on this computer: http://localhost:8080/.

How to use it

  • Select a sentence on any web page, right-click, and choose Check this claim with Evaluator. Evaluator opens with the claim filled in; press Support, Challenge, Evaluate or Verify.
  • Right-click a page or a link and choose Find claims on this page to have Evaluator pick out the claims worth checking.
  • From the toolbar button, Find claims in a screenshot of this tab works for images and posts too (your AI model must be able to read pictures).
