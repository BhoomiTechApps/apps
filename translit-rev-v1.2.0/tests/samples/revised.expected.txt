e prithibir‌ma kale kale nanan ghotona ghote zar‌ga. ou ghotona uta du:khomoy oiteu pare baro anondomoy oiteu pare. du:kho baro hotashar srishti oile srijon‌shil lekhok ba lekhika jorom oya mingal‌pa poth‌ il‌korediya uhanor somadhan ahan kore ditara.
 
iurope somoy ahan das protha cholesil. manure bajare niya bech‌la. guru, moish‌, sagir sade manure byobohar kor‌la. ou du:khomoy tan‌ja ugot tanur du:khor, tapitwor sobi uhan prithibir manurang tuliya dhoresili amerikar lekhika agoi. teir nang‌ — heriyet elijabeth‌ bichar shto.
 
leng‌kora lerik‌hanor nang‌ -- angkol tom‌s‌ kebin. ou lerik uhane iurope biplob anesil. bibhinno deshe ain pash‌ koriya das protha nishiddho koresila. sahityor bhumika somoye somoye esade oya prokash‌ or.
 
amar bharot‌borshor swadhinota biplobe bongkim‌chondro girokor “anondomoth‌” baro kobi noj‌rul is‌lamor “ognibina”  manure thoutal desil. anondomothor - “bondematorom‌” dhwoni biplobe jagoronor joyar anesil. esade kore prithibir bibhinno deshe sahityik girok‌gasiye kolomor madhyome biplob anesila.
 
amar hurukang‌ jat ehanor somaj bibortone sor‌karorangto dabi aday kor‌tega somaje bohu onoikyor srishti osil ehan habiye har‌pasihan. pode pode bibhedor srishti osil; ere niyom ehan bare bare deha dile somaje bhobishyot unnoti niyam hinoya zitoiga. bortomane prithibit hurukang‌ jator bhasha, songskriti niyam twora oya mimut oite ase. ere bidhwongsi hut egot manu zodi atmogyani naitara uhan oile kakeihan lehe bipod‌.
 
ere obosthat mor ikora lerik shou ehan zodi manur hridit zodishoi mingal ahan dena parer uhan oile lerik‌shou ehanor sarthokota bichar oyotoi. ek‌matro pathok girigithanirang ehanor mulyayon onar bhar‌han thagel‌ga.
