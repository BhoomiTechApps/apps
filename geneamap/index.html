Relationship finder

Pick two people to see how they are related. Tip: select two people on the map before opening this.

Tree check

Every link is checked against family logic: nobody can be their own ancestor, a child has at most two parents, parents are born before their children, close relatives can’t be partners, and siblings share a generation. Problems are blocked when you connect people; warnings are allowed but unusual.

Kinship terms

Relationships are worked out in English. Add the words your community uses for relatives, and they appear in the Relationship finder and in the kinship view on the tree. Each word is written against a kin code, the relationship spelt out step by step: FeB is father’s elder brother, MB mother’s brother.

Choose a list of terms, or start a new one. A saved tree file carries the list in use, so others who open it see the same words.

This is a starter list made as an example. Check every word with elders of your community and correct it, or press Copy and build your own from it. Usage differs between regions, communities and families.

How to write kin codes

Spell the relationship from the person asking (Person A) to the relative, one step at a time:

F fatherM motherP parent
S sonD daughterC child
B brotherZ sisterG brother or sister
H husbandW wifeE husband or wife

e or y after B, Z or G: elder or younger than the person before it. FeB father’s elder brother, FyB father’s younger brother, eZ my elder sister.

+ or - at the end: the relative is older or younger than Person A. FBS+ father’s brother’s son, older than me.

ms: or ws: at the start: the word a man or a woman uses, where they differ. ws:BS brother’s son, said by a woman.

P, C, G and E cover both genders, so PF is any grandfather and EM either spouse’s mother. When several codes fit, the most specific one is used. If a list tells elder from younger but the ages are not known, both words are shown; add birth years, or the birth order under More details, to settle it.

The Relationship finder shows the code for any two people, with a button to add a word for it.
