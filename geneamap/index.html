Tree Check

Every link is checked against family logic: nobody can be their own ancestor, a child has at most two parents, parents are born before their children, close relatives can’t be partners, and siblings share a generation. ⛔ Problems are blocked when you connect people; ⚠ warnings are allowed but unusual.
